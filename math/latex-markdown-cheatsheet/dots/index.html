LaTeX dots and ellipses
…\Large \ldots…    \ldots\Large \verb!\ldots!\ldots (baseline dots)
⋯\Large \cdots⋯ \cdots\Large \verb!\cdots!\cdots (centred dots)
⋮\Large \vdots⋮ \vdots\Large \verb!\vdots!\vdots (vertical)
⋱\Large \ddots⋱ \ddots\Large \verb!\ddots!\ddots (diagonal)
…\Large \dots… \dots\Large \verb!\dots!\dots (context-sensitive)
1…n\Large 1 \dotsc n1…n 1 \dotsc n\Large \verb!1 \dotsc n!1 \dotsc n (with commas)
a⋯b\Large a \dotsb ba⋯b a \dotsb b\Large \verb!a \dotsb b!a \dotsb b (with binary ops)
A ⁣⋯B\Large A \dotsi BA⋯B A \dotsi B\Large \verb!A \dotsi B!A \dotsi B (with integrals)

\dots picks the right variant from context; the explicit forms are useful when the context is ambiguous. \cdots is also used between matrix entries — see matrices-and-arrays.
