Quantifiers and connectives
∀x\Large \forall x∀x    \forall x\Large \verb!\forall x!\forall x (for all)
∃x\Large \exists x∃x \exists x\Large \verb!\exists x!\exists x (there exists)
∄x\Large \nexists x∄x \nexists x\Large \verb!\nexists x!\nexists x (does not exist)
¬p\Large \neg p¬p \neg p\Large \verb!\neg p!\neg p (not; also \lnot)
p∧q\Large p \land qp∧q p \land q\Large \verb!p \land q!p \land q (and)
p∨q\Large p \lor qp∨q p \lor q\Large \verb!p \lor q!p \lor q (or)
p  ⟹  q\Large p \implies qp⟹q p \implies q\Large \verb!p \implies q!p \implies q (implies)
p  ⟺  q\Large p \iff qp⟺q p \iff q\Large \verb!p \iff q!p \iff q (if and only if)

\iff is the logical biconditional (⟺); \leftrightarrow is only a bidirectional arrow and lives in arrows. \therefore (“therefore”) is a distinct symbol from \implies.
