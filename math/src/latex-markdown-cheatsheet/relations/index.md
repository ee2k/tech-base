Type commands, not pasted Unicode (`≤`, `≥`, `≈`, `∝`): commands remain portable
across renderers. Equivalence and similarity relations (`\approx`, `\equiv`,
`\sim`, `\simeq`, `\cong`) are in
[logic-and-proofs](../logic-and-proofs/); `\subset` and `\subseteq` are
in [set-theory](../set-theory/).
