`\mathbb`, `\mathcal`, `\mathbf` and `\mathrm` have a single home here; they are
no longer duplicated in fractions-and-radicals/ or accents/. For labelled
operators built from text (`\operatorname{Var}`) see
[functions-and-operators](../functions-and-operators/); for `\text{...}`
and escaping see [text-spacing-escaping](../text-spacing-escaping/).
